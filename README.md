## 🇬🇧 About

Instead of a plain résumé, I designed a **Minecraft-style 3D world** where visitors walk around and step into portals to explore who I am and what I build. The world, character, lighting, audio and interactions are all hand-written from scratch.

### Highlights
- Procedurally generated floating block island
- Five portals (About · Skills · Portfolio · Experience · Contact), enterable from both sides
- Pixel-art blocky character with walk animation
- Day / night mode, generative background music (Web Audio)
- Full touch controls, bilingual FA / EN with RTL

### Tech
`Three.js` · `Vite` · `Vanilla JavaScript` · `Web Audio API` · `CSS`
